Dopamine Banking Framework

By forcing yourself to "earn" your distractions, you gamify dopamine pathways. Work becomes the pathway to freedom, and relaxation becomes a scarce, valuable resource.

  • ✔ Earn Ratio (e.g. 1 to 0.5): 1 minute of hard work earns you 30 seconds of relaxation.
  • ✔ Double Tracking: Accumulate balance when doing positive habits, then flip the trigger when you start scrolling or resting.
  • ✔ Zero Out Action: If your break balance hits exactly zero, the app automatically triggers an audio alarm to tell you to stop.
  • ✔ Daily Reset: Every single day, the tracker resets back to zero so you start fresh without carrying over massive balances.
