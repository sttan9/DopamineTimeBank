Dopamine Banking Framework

By forcing yourself to "earn" your distractions, you gamify dopamine pathways. Work becomes the pathway to freedom, and relaxation becomes a scarce, valuable resource.

  • ✔ Earn Ratio: 1 minute of hard work earns you a configured fraction of relaxation (e.g. 30 seconds if set to 0.5).
  • ✔ State Restoring Persistence: Close your browser anytime. The app logs start and end times to calculate missing time if you reboot.
  • ✔ Daily Auto-Reset: At midnight, today's timers automatically reset and get archived to your History Log.
